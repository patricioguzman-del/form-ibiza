# 01 · Brand DNA: FORM | ibiza

**Site audited:** formibiza.com (Wix) · **Date:** 15 Nov 2026
**Pages seen:** Home, Sales, Rentals, Developers, About, two listings (Dragonfly, Can Draco), the menu, the contact popup, the footer.

**Read this first: what was and wasn't checked**
- Desktop only. The browser window would not shrink below 1512px wide, and Wix serves phones a separate layout, so **mobile is not audited**. Every size below was measured at a 1512px-wide window.
- **MEASURED** = read from the page code. **ESTIMATED** = judged by eye from screenshots.
- Page speed was not measured.

---

## 1. The feeling

**One sentence:** A quiet, private advisor's notebook: warm paper, one italic serif voice, a dark ink colour, and photographs given room, with nothing asking for attention.

**It is:** discreet · warm · literary · unhurried · architectural
**It is not:** flashy · salesy · portal-like · glossy · corporate

---

## 2. Typography

Three typefaces. All names are MEASURED (read from inside the font files).

| Role | Typeface | Details (MEASURED) |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | **Didot** Regular | "FORM \| ibiza", 24px, letter-spacing 4.8px (0.2em), black. Footer version: 21px, "FORM" bolder, "ibiza" regular, in cream. |
| Headings | **ITC Cheltenham Std**, mainly **Book Italic** | 21.3px, letter-spacing 0.64px (0.03em), line-height up to 1.8, colour #100e1a. Sentence case, never uppercase. |
| Headings, secondary | ITC Cheltenham Std **Light Italic**, **Book**, **Light** | "The Location" 24px Light Italic. Prices about 17.7px italic. Spec labels 12–13px. |
| Body | **Avenir LT 35 Light** | 14px, line-height 1.8 to 2.2 (25–31px). Captions 12px. Property tags 12px with 0.72px tracking in #2b2b2b. |
| Buttons | Avenir LT 35 Light | 11.8 to 16px depending on the button. |

**What the typography does emotionally**
- The italic Cheltenham is the brand's voice. It reads like a handwritten note from a person, not a company headline. This is the single most recognisable thing on the site.
- Headings are small (21px). The site never raises its voice. The confidence comes from the restraint.
- Light Avenir with very open line spacing gives the "air" and calm.
- Wide-spaced Didot in the logo adds a fashion and editorial note.

**Flaws to fix quietly**
- Headings are set to "bold", but only the Book weight is loaded, so the browser fakes the bold. The result is slightly smudged letters. The same happens on bold Avenir.
- Four Cheltenham cuts are used with no clear rule. Spec labels on one listing mix two of them.
- Button and body text sizes vary from page to page for no reason.

**Licence warning for the rebuild:** ITC Cheltenham and Avenir are commercial fonts. Wix supplies Avenir under its own licence, which won't carry over to a self-hosted site. The Didot file is a third-party version (credited to "Double Alex Font Studio"). Budget for web font licences, or confirm what the owner already holds. I'm not a lawyer, so have this checked.

- Terracotta appears once, in the menu. It's the one "Ibiza earth" note.
- Thin grey divider lines separate sections (colour ESTIMATED as mid-grey, about 1px).

## 4. Space & layout

- **Content column:** 1118px in a 1512px window, so about 74% of the width with 13% margins each side (MEASURED).
- **Framed, not full-bleed:** hero images sit inside the margins like a print in a mount. Ratio about 1.9:1 (1118×591, MEASURED). One full-width image band per page is the exception.
- **Listing cards:** two columns, images 3:2 (549×366, MEASURED), gap about 20px (ESTIMATED). Under each image: name and location on the left, price and bedrooms on the right, then a thin rule.
- **Editorial pages (About, Developers):** asymmetric. A narrow text column on one side, a tall portrait-format photo on the other.
- **White space:** very generous. About 100–150px between sections (ESTIMATED).
- **Corners:** all square. Buttons have 0 radius (MEASURED). No shadows, no cards, no icons apart from the menu lines and gallery arrows.
- **Header:** 120px tall, three-line menu icon on the left, logo centred. It scrolls away with the page (MEASURED: not sticky).
- **Why it feels architectural:** straight edges, a strict column, thin rules, and images treated as framed objects.

---

## 5. Photography & imagery

- **Brand image:** a black-and-white, high-contrast photo of Es Vedrà. It opens the home page and closes the About page. It's the emotional anchor of the brand.
- **Property photos:** full colour, natural light, mostly golden hour and dusk. Pools, terraces, sea horizons, interiors with warm materials. No people.
- **About:** one portrait of a man with a dog against a pale plaster wall. Natural, relaxed, unposed. It carries a lot of the "independent, personal" feeling.
- **Location map:** each listing has a custom dark silhouette of the island with a single cream dot for the property. It's a small signature element and worth keeping.
- **Weakness:** the quality of property photos varies. Some are editorial, some look like standard agent or drone shots. The saturated blues of some pool shots fight with the muted palette.

---

## 6. Voice & copy

- Short, declarative sentences. Often two short lines as a heading.
- Words that recur: discretion, independent, considered, off-market, intelligence, quietly.
- It says what it won't do as much as what it will: a limited number of buyer searches, only part of the market shown publicly.
- It names architects and designers (Chipperfield, Pawson, Blakstad) as tags instead of using superlatives.
- No exclamation marks, no "luxury", no "dream home".
- Example: *"On or off market. Always with discretion."*
- The Sales intro makes the same move: it tells the visitor that the best homes sell privately and that the page shows only a small part.
- Listing descriptions are longer and more conventional than the rest of the site. They lean on "spectacular" and "magnificent", which the brand pages avoid.

---

## 7. Motion & interaction

- Text and images fade in softly as you scroll (observed).
- Button and link transitions take 0.4s (MEASURED). Slow and calm.
- Listing cards have a small photo carousel with dots and arrows.
- The menu opens as a full-screen paper-coloured sheet with a short list of italic links.
- Contact opens as a popup over a dimmed page.
- Overall pace: slow, no parallax, no autoplay drama.

---

## 8. The DNA list: non-negotiables for the rebuild

1. Warm paper background **#f8f7f3**. Never pure white.
2. Ink **#100e1a** for text, buttons and footer. Never pure black.
3. **ITC Cheltenham Italic** for headings, in sentence case.
4. Headings stay small and quiet. No giant hero type.
5. **Avenir Light** body text with very open line spacing.
6. The **Didot** wordmark, letter-spaced and centred, with the minimal three-line menu.
7. Images **framed inside wide margins**, with only an occasional full-width band.
8. Square corners, thin rules, no shadows, no icons, no badges.
9. The **black-and-white Es Vedrà** photograph as the brand image.
10. The copy: short, calm, discretion-first. Off-market framing. Architects named.
11. One whisper of **terracotta**, used almost nowhere.
12. The island silhouette map with a single dot.
13. Slow, soft fades. Nothing bounces, slides or autoplays.

## 9. What's holding it back from 10/10

**Vibe risk:** how likely the fix is to damage the feeling.

| # | Problem (all observed on the live site) | Vibe risk |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Template leftovers.** The LinkedIn icon in the menu links to Wix's own company page. Hidden placeholder text sits in the listing page code ("Lorem ipsum", "Your Video Title"). | None |
| 2 | **Data errors.** One price is written "€8.750,000". "Secruity" is misspelled. One price is "€7,777,777", which looks like a placeholder. The same village appears as San Josep, Sant Josep and Sant Josep de sa Talaia. | None |
| 3 | **No contact page.** Contact is a popup titled "Request info for this property", and it shows that title even on the Developers page. No email address, no office address, no named person. | Low |
| 4 | **No legal pages found.** No privacy policy, legal notice or cookie information was linked in the footer or on the listing page tested, while the site collects emails. For a Spanish business this needs checking with a lawyer. | None |
| 5 | **About page has no name, no team, no track record.** For UHNW clients, who they deal with is the product. | Low |
| 6 | **Rentals has no properties**, only a paragraph and a button. **Developers has no call to action** at all. | Low |
| 7 | **Search.** Sales is one long scroll of 20 homes with only a "Sort by". No filters for area, price or bedrooms, no map. | Medium: filters must stay very quiet |
| 8 | **Listing pages are thin on facts.** Five specs only. No reference number, no licence or energy data, no clear gallery count. A video block showed as a plain black rectangle while scrolling. | Low |
| 9 | **SEO basics missing.** No meta description on any page checked. Ten H1 headings on a listing page. 29 of 31 listing images have no alt text. Image file names appear as page text. The sitemap lists 23 listings while the Sales page shows 20. | None |
| 10 | **English only.** No Spanish or other language version. | None |
| 11 | **Header disappears** on long pages, so there's no way to navigate without scrolling back up. | Low: a slim, quiet sticky bar is fine |
| 12 | **Text is small and light** (12–14px, Light weight). Elegant, but hard to read for many people over 50, which is much of the audience. | Medium: raise sizes one step, keep the spacing |
| 13 | **Typographic inconsistencies** (faked bold, mixed cuts, varying sizes). | None: fixing it strengthens the vibe |
| 14 | **Uneven photography.** | Low |
| 15 | **Lead capture is one generic form.** No distinction between buyer, seller, rental and developer enquiries. Newsletter is the only other capture. | Low |
| 16 | **No outside analytics detected** on the one listing page tested (Wix may track internally). | None |

**Not checked:** mobile layout, page speed, the image gallery, the brochure and floor-plan downloads, where the forms send.

## 10. Benchmarks

From my own knowledge of these sites, not re-checked today.

- **The Modern House** (UK): how to make a calm, filterable property search that still feels like a magazine. Also their writing on each home.
- **Inigo** (UK): historic homes sold with editorial storytelling and consistent photography.
- **Fantastic Frank** (Stockholm/Europe): one strict photographic style across every listing, so the whole site feels art-directed.
- **Aman** (hospitality): restraint at the very top of the market. Few words, large calm images, total consistency.
- **Cereal magazine**: the type-and-white-space discipline that FORM is already close to.

## Five questions for the owner before designing

1. Which fonts does he hold licences for (Cheltenham, Avenir, Didot), and is he open to close alternatives if licensing is costly?
2. How much should the site show: all listings publicly, or a gated off-market section for registered clients?
3. Who is the face of FORM? Is he willing to be named, pictured and quoted on the About page?
4. Which languages matter for his clients: Spanish, French, German, Dutch?
5. Where should enquiries go, and which tools must connect later (CRM, portals, WhatsApp, newsletter)?
